## 🎓 Research Information

- **Programme:** Taiwan Experience Education Program (TEEP)
- **University:** Feng Chia University, Taiwan
- **Department:** Department of Electronics
- **Laboratory:** Reliable Integrated Circuit Development Laboratory
- **Research period:** September–December 2026

## 🚀 Research Topic

**Machine Learning-Based Early Prediction of Routing Congestion and Self-Heating Hotspots in ASAP7 FinFET Physical Design**

The project explores the potential use of machine learning to predict routing congestion and self-heating hotspots early in the physical-design flow.

## 🎯 Research Motivation

- Routing congestion can contribute to routing difficulties, design-rule violations and timing degradation.
- FinFET self-heating can affect device performance and reliability.
- Detecting potential hotspots earlier may help inform physical-design optimization.

## 🧪 Proposed Research Workflow

1. Initial placement of the benchmark design.
2. Extraction of physical-design and activity-related features.
3. Development of suitable congestion and self-heating reference data.
4. Investigation of machine-learning-based hotspot prediction.
5. Exploration of placement refinement and physical-design evaluation.

## 🖥️ Proposed Benchmark

The introductory presentation identifies an **8-bit low-power processor** as the benchmark design, with an intended evaluation of congestion, self-heating, timing, power and area.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

NGSPICE · ASAP7 · Yosys · OpenROAD · KLayout · Machine Learning
